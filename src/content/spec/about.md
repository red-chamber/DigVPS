# About

I am Cedrian, a computer science student at HUST, drawn to the quiet machinery beneath visible things. I collect questions at the borders of code, systems, and imperfect digital worlds—where a broken model may reveal more than a flawless one, and every terminal is a doorway. This site is a small observatory for what I learn, build, and deliberately leave unfinished. If you have found it, perhaps you were meant to.
