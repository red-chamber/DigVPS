# Entry Protocol

You have reached the outer edge of Cedrian Ian.

Nothing here is arranged for comfort. Notes are fragments recovered from unfinished systems, abandoned experiments, and questions that refused to disappear.

Use **Notes** to enter the archive.  
Use **Timeline** to trace what was created, altered, or erased.  
Use **About** if you insist on knowing who left these records behind.  
Your **Account** is the only proof that the system recognizes you.

Some entries are incomplete. Some paths end without warning. What has been deleted will not return.

Proceed quietly. Read carefully.

The archive remembers more than it reveals.
